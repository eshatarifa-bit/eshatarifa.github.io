About

I am Eshat Jahan Arifa, a Biomedical Engineering graduate from Chittagong University of Engineering & Technology (CUET). I completed my B.Sc. in June 2026 with a CGPA of 3.62/4.00. My undergraduate thesis developed a gelatin–sodium alginate hydrogel loaded with Calendula officinalis extract as a wound dressing.

Since July 2026, I have been a Research Assistant in the Electrochemical Lab at CUET, supervised by Dr. Sumit Majumder and Dr. Arup Kumer Roy. I conduct electrochemical measurements and analyze the responses for hydrocortisone detection, using cyclic voltammetry and potentiostat operation. This work is in progress. I also have two published IEEE conference papers, on EEG-based emotion detection and Parkinson's detection, and I am a member of BrainRot Lab, a brain–computer interface research group.

My broader interest is in the materials that connect the body to electronics. I want to bring biomaterials and biosignal processing together in work on soft, biocompatible materials for neural interfaces and brain–computer interfaces, and on how material properties affect the quality of the signals we record.

I am actively seeking a funded PhD position starting Fall 2027, combining biomaterials and brain–computer interfaces. If you are a professor working in these areas, I would love to connect.

Research Interests

Biomaterials and Neural Interfaces

Soft, biocompatible materials such as hydrogels, and how their properties could shape the stability and quality of recorded biosignals.

Biosignal Processing and Machine Learning

Feature extraction and machine learning for EEG and other biomedical signals, from emotion detection to Parkinson's detection.

Electrochemical Sensing

Characterizing sensing interfaces with cyclic voltammetry and potentiostat measurements, currently through hydrocortisone detection.
